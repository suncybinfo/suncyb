About Us

At Suncyb Infotech, we are a forward-thinking IT company dedicated to delivering innovative, reliable, and customized technology solutions. With a strong focus on quality and client satisfaction, we specialize in developing ERP systems, web applications, and enterprise software that drive digital transformation and operational efficiency.

Our expertise spans across multiple industries, with a special emphasis on government skilling initiatives. Our flagship product, a powerful Skilling ERP platform, is exclusively designed to support training centers and institutions involved in national and state-level vocational training programs.

ERP is Only for Government Skilling

At Suncyb Infotech, we are fully dedicated to supporting India’s mission of skill development through government-backed initiatives. Our ERP solution is exclusively built for government skilling programs, designed to meet the unique operational, reporting, and compliance needs of training centers and institutions working under national and state-level schemes.

    Key Focus Areas:
  • Programs like PMKVY, DDU-GKY, NSDC, NULM, and various state skilling missions
  • Streamlined processes for trainee registration, batch formation, trainer assignment, and attendance tracking
  • Automated assessment, certification, and placement tracking
  • Real-time reporting and compliance tools aligned with government guidelines
  • Ensuring transparency, accountability, and operational efficiency in every step of the training cycle

By focusing solely on the government skilling ecosystem, our ERP delivers targeted, reliable,and compliant solutions that help institutions stay aligned with scheme-specific requirements and contribute to India’s vision of a Skilled, Empowered Nation.

 

About Portal

At Suncyb Infotech, we specialize in delivering advanced Skilling ERP solutions designed to support a wide range of government schemes and initiatives in vocational training and skill development.

Our ERP system is built to manage and streamline operations for training centers, institutes, and organizations participating in flagship programs like PMKVY, DDU-GKY, NSDC, NULM, and other state-level skilling missions.

Our platform offers end-to-end functionality for trainee enrollment, batch scheduling, trainer management, attendance tracking, assessment, certification, and placement tracking. With an intuitive interface and scalable structure, we empower skilling institutions to enhance transparency, accountability, and efficiency at every stage of the training lifecycle.
